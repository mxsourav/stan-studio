Print and build notes
  • Print in black or dark PLA/PETG so the panels block light fully. Panels print flat, stencil face up is the inside.
  • Stand the panels around the floor rim: top and bottom are the long ones, left and right fit between. Glue the corners and hang by the hole near the top.
  • Put a 5 mm warm-white LED in the socket at the top of the tube and wire it down through the floor.
  • Print the box picture plate in black and glue it on a light floor.
  • Thin struts hold floating pieces and show as thin radial lines in the shadow.

Figure

Box centre X and Box centre Y (0 to 100%, default 50) say where the box sits inside your picture. The part of the figure right around the box is hidden behind the box itself, and the status line tells you what share of the figure that is. Move the centre onto a part you can lose, such as a body or plain background, rather than a face.

Figure size runs from 40 to 110% of the maximum reach of the panels. At 100% the far edge of the figure just fits; above that, the outer parts fall past the panel tops and show up in the Figure lost above panel top stat. Lower it until that stat is near 0%.

Shadow width and Shadow length (50 to 2500 mm) let you type the size you want on the wall. If the box cannot reach it, the tool lowers the LED toward the panel tops and then raises the panels, and tells you the heights it used. Your sliders are not changed, so Back to auto size or moving Figure size returns to your own values. Mirror horizontally flips the figure, and Use the background as the figure swaps shape and background.

Box shape and size

Rectangle and Diamond take a width and a height; the other shapes take one diameter. Round has 12 flat walls, while Cylinder is a single round wall. Inner width runs from 60 to 200 mm and inner height from 60 to 220 mm.

  • Panel max height (20 to 200 mm, default 48) is one of the two settings that decide shadow size. Taller panels reach further up the wall.
  • LED height (30 to 220 mm, default 62) sets the tube height. The closer the LED is to the panel tops, the bigger the shadow. It is always kept at least 6 mm above the panels.
  • Panel thickness (1.2 to 3 mm): the default 1.6 mm suits most printers. Go thicker for a stiffer panel on a large box.
  • Floor thickness (2 to 25 mm, default 3) is fine at 3 mm for most lamps.
  • Strut width (0.6 to 3 mm, default 0.8) sets the thin bridges that hold floating pieces, like the inside of an eye, to the rest of the panel. Thin struts hide better in the shadow but are weaker.
  • Resolution is the grid the stencil is cut on: Draft 0.3, Good 0.2, Fine 0.15 or Ultra 0.1 mm. Use Draft to try ideas, Fine or Ultra for the final file.

Reading the previews and stats

In the wall preview, dark areas are shadow from the panels and amber marks parts of the figure where light leaks through because no panel covers them. Leaks at the outer edge usually go away when you lower Figure size. The 3D view switches between Assembled, Print layout and Real light. Floating detail counts the pieces joined by struts; dropped pieces were too small to print and were left out. Weight assumes solid plastic, so your slicer will show less with infill.

Converting a photo or PNG

Any image that is not an SVG opens the converter first. Threshold (default 41) decides which pixels become shape: raise it to keep only the darkest areas, lower it to include lighter greys. Automatic threshold picks a value for you. Smoothing (0 to 4) softens jagged edges, Path simplify reduces points, and Remove specks deletes small islands. Halftone turns grey tones into a dot pattern (Dot spacing 5 to 20); use Solid for logos and silhouettes.
